SLICK: Lunch on the Lam

Slick the rabbit has a nose for lunch and a whole town on his tail. Tap the right side of the screen to jump, hold the left side to duck, and run for it.

Help

  • Too hard for little ones? Pause the game and turn on Little ones mode: a slower chase, a shield that grows back, and automatic ducking.
  • Game Center trophies not showing? Make sure you are signed in under Settings, then Game Center. Trophies earned while signed out are sent the next time you open the game signed in.
  • Where are my share cards? In the Files app, under On My iPhone, then Slick.
  • Language: the game follows your device language (English, French, Spanish, Portuguese or German). You can change it from the pause menu.
