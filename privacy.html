Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026. SLICK: Lunch on the Lam is a single-player game made by Simple Art Games, a studio of Ouzatech. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, analytics, usage data, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera input, or advertising identifiers. There are no accounts, no ads, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps your progress, carrots, medals, trophies, meals, outfits, settings and your best Daily Dash run in a save file on your device. Deleting the app removes it. If you save a Daily Dash share card, the picture is saved to the game's folder in the Files app on your device, and only you decide where it goes from there.

Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores (your best distance and best score in endless mode) are sent to Apple's Game Center service. Depending on your Game Center settings, your Game Center nickname and scores can be visible to other players. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple Account, email or contact details. The game works fully without Game Center, and its trophies are also shown inside the game. You can sign out in Settings, then Game Center.

Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads. Apple processes App Store downloads and payments under its own privacy policy; we never receive payment details.

Children

The game is made for all ages, including young children, and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no chat and no way to contact other players. Settings includes a link to this privacy policy.

Contact

If you email engineering@ouzatech.com, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you, and we do not sell or share them. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the game's next release.
